Warm-weather travel with real variety

The Caribbean and Latin America sit close to the DC area, but they are anything but similar. Cuba offers colonial towns and classic cars. The Dominican Republic offers big, easy resorts. Costa Rica offers wildlife and waterfalls. Choosing well matters, and that is where an advisor helps.

At Luxury World Travel we match each place to the traveler. Families often want kids clubs and connecting rooms. Couples want quiet beaches and good restaurants. Groups want space, activities and simple pricing. We compare resorts honestly and explain the trade-offs.

Many of these trips need extra care, from travel documents to local transfers and weather timing. We take care of it before you leave and stay reachable while you are away, so you can enjoy the trip instead of managing it.

Frequently asked questions

Which Caribbean island is best for families?

The Dominican Republic, the Bahamas and Barbados are popular for family-friendly resorts and calm beaches. The best fit depends on ages and interests.

When should I avoid the Caribbean?

Hurricane season runs from June to November, with the highest risk in late summer and early autumn. We can still plan trips, with flexible terms and insurance guidance.

Can I combine beach time and adventure?

Yes. Costa Rica is the classic example, but we can pair a resort stay with excursions almost anywhere in the region.

Do you plan all-inclusive vacations?

Yes. We compare all-inclusive resorts by food quality, room standard and activities, not just price.

Do you help with travel insurance?

We provide advice on travel insurance so you understand your options. Please read policy terms carefully before buying.
